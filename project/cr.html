When to Write a Community Report

  • Policy violations
  • Roommate conflict/mediations
  • Concerning Behavior
  • Mental Health situations
  • Certain maintenance issues
  • Any resident shares a concern
  • Anything that makes you stop and notice!

Details to Include for Alcohol Incidents

  • Names and quantities of each type of alcohol present:
    • 1 half-full Bootlegger bottle
    • 1 full Corona beer bottle
    • 1 empty single bottle of 19 Crimes wine
    • 5 empty White Claw cans of various flavors
  • Who is taking responsibility for it:
    • Bruce Banner took responsibility for all alcohol in the room.
  • For each resident present, the answers to "if they are 21 or over?" and "did you drink?":
    • Resident Bruce Banner, under the age of 21, “yes” to drinking.
    • Resident Steve Rogers, under the age of 21, “no” to drinking.
  • Who poured out the alcohol after the RA escorted them to a sink:
    • Resident Tony Stark poured out all alcohol and threw away the empties.
