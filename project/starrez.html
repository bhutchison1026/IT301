What is StarRez?

StarRez is the student housing management software that JMU Residence Life uses. They have both a website and phone application with similar features! You can find more information about the StarRez software here: StarRez.

What can I use it for?

StarRez is most useful for finding information related to specific residents, dorm buildings, or dorm rooms. Resident Advisers and Hall Directors are able to create reports relating to specific students and events they're connected with. This helps keep a database of information that can be used in the future by other departments who need information to help students.

How do I navigate it?

StarRez is pretty intuitive to use! The side bar on the left of the screen helps users navigate to the different sections that are discussed below:

The dashboard can be customized for each user based on what they need! StarRez offers pre-built panels that users can adjust to fit their needs and display information quickly on their home page. If none of the pre-built panels match the user's needs, custom panels can be made using SQL queries!

The main panel holds information relating to residents on campus. You can search for a specific person based on their name, their room, their id, etc.

The community panel grants access to different forms that a user can write regarding residents. The most common forms used are community reports, ICON reports, and program requests!
